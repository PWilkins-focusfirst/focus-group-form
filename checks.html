Office Depot B 7200 business voucher checks (item 637‑515) · South State Bank

Account & Bank Setup

These are blank check sheets, so the app prints the full check face including the MICR line. Banks technically require magnetic MICR toner for reliable machine reading — standard toner usually clears via image processing, but confirm with South State.
Advanced: stock dimensions, calibration & MICR
Perforation layout — height of the check portion (top) and each voucher stub. Office Depot 637‑515 default is 3.5" each.
Printer calibration — if everything prints shifted, nudge the whole page. Positive X = right, positive Y = down. Print the alignment guide, hold it over a real check against a light, and adjust.
MICR line — position & size of the magnetic number line. It must sit inside the clear band (bottom 0.625" of the check). Leave ~0.6" blank at the right edge for the bank's amount field.
